# SugarTrack Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 10, 2026

SugarTrack is a glucose tracking application designed to help users record, review, and better understand their glucose readings.

This Privacy Policy explains what information SugarTrack handles, how that information is stored and used, and what choices users have regarding their data.

---

## 1. Information SugarTrack Handles

SugarTrack allows users to enter health-related information, including:

- Blood glucose readings
- Measurement context, such as fasting, before meals, after meals, or bedtime
- Optional notes associated with glucose readings
- Dates and times associated with readings

SugarTrack also stores application preferences, including:

- Custom glucose target ranges
- Theme preference
- Dashboard visibility preferences

---

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information entered into SugarTrack is used only to provide the app's features, including:

- Displaying glucose readings
- Showing glucose history
- Calculating summaries and trends
- Calculating Time in Range
- Providing weekly insights and context analytics
- Tracking logging streaks
- Classifying glucose readings according to configured target ranges
- Creating CSV exports
- Creating PDF reports

SugarTrack does not use health information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or sale.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage

SugarTrack is designed as a local-first application.

Glucose readings and associated information are stored locally on the user's Android device using the Room database system.

Application preferences are stored locally using Android Preferences DataStore.

SugarTrack currently does not require a SugarTrack account and does not upload glucose readings to a SugarTrack-operated cloud service.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

SugarTrack does not automatically transmit glucose readings or related health information to third parties.

The app allows users to create CSV files and PDF reports.

These files are shared only when the user chooses to use Android's sharing functionality.

When a user shares an exported file, the selected external application or service may process that file according to its own privacy policy.

Users should review the privacy practices of any third-party application or service they choose to use for sharing.

---

## 5. Data Collection by the Developer

The current version of SugarTrack does not intentionally collect or transmit users' glucose readings, notes, measurement contexts, or other health information to the developer.

SugarTrack currently does not use its own analytics or advertising service to collect health information.

If SugarTrack's data practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the application's disclosures will be updated before those changes are introduced.

---

## 6. Health Information

Glucose information is sensitive health information.

SugarTrack uses this information solely to provide features requested by the user.

SugarTrack does not sell health information.

SugarTrack does not use health information for targeted advertising.

SugarTrack does not use health information to create advertising profiles.

---

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Glucose readings remain stored on the user's device until the user deletes them or the application's local data is removed.

Users can delete individual glucose readings using SugarTrack's available delete functionality.

Removing SugarTrack's application data through Android system settings or uninstalling the application may remove locally stored SugarTrack data.

Users should export any information they wish to retain before clearing application data or uninstalling SugarTrack.

---

## 8. Security

SugarTrack is designed to minimize unnecessary transmission of sensitive health information by storing glucose data locally on the device.

The application uses Android platform storage and application sandbox protections.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security on their device, including screen locks, operating-system updates, and controlling access to exported files.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

---

## 9. Permissions

SugarTrack seeks to request only permissions necessary for its functionality.

The current application does not require broad storage permission to create or share CSV and PDF reports.

Files intended for sharing are provided through Android's secure file-sharing mechanisms.

If future features require additional sensitive permissions, SugarTrack will explain their purpose and update this Privacy Policy where appropriate.

---

## 10. Health Connect

SugarTrack currently does not access or store data through Android Health Connect.

If Health Connect support is added in the future, SugarTrack will provide additional disclosures and comply with applicable Google Play and Android Health Connect requirements before requesting access.

---

## 11. Medical Disclaimer

SugarTrack is intended to help users record and review glucose information.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise in applicable regulatory documentation, SugarTrack is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical care.

Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding medical decisions or concerns about glucose readings.

If a user believes they are experiencing a medical emergency, they should seek appropriate emergency medical assistance.

---

## 12. Children's Privacy

SugarTrack is not specifically directed toward children.

The app does not intentionally collect personal information from children through a SugarTrack-operated online service.

Parents or guardians are responsible for determining whether use of the application is appropriate for a minor under their supervision.

---

## 13. Third-Party Software

SugarTrack is built using Android and open-source software libraries.

Those libraries may be subject to their own licenses.

SugarTrack's use of third-party software does not grant those software providers automatic access to locally stored glucose readings.

If future versions integrate third-party online services, those integrations and their data practices will be disclosed here.

---

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as SugarTrack evolves.

Changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and publishing the revised policy.

Material changes affecting how health or personal information is handled will be disclosed as appropriate before or when those changes take effect.
